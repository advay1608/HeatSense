Meteorological Context: NWP Grid vs. IMD Mumbai Stations

Open-Meteo operational NWP grid cell (19.0861°N, 72.8529°E, El. 6m) predicts 36.8–37.6°C during active post-monsoon "October Heat". Clear skies and warm continental easterly winds delay the sea breeze across inland/suburban Mumbai, matching IMD Santacruz observations (typical highs 36.0–37.8°C; all-time October record 38.6°C). In contrast, coastal IMD Colaba is moderated on three sides by marine airflow (typical highs 32.5–34.5°C). The NWP grid represents suburban areal thermodynamics rather than point thermometer shelters. HeatSense preserves authentic raw API values without artificial modification.
